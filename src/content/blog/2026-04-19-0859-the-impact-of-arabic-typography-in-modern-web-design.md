## The Challenges of Arabic Typography
Arabic script is written from right to left, which can pose challenges for designers who are accustomed to working with left-to-right scripts. Additionally, Arabic characters have unique calligraphic features, such as ligatures and diacritical marks, that require special attention to detail. To overcome these challenges, designers must have a deep understanding of Arabic typography and its nuances.

## The Importance of Arabic Typography in Web Design
