Arabic typography is unique and requires special attention to detail. The Arabic script is written from right to left, which can pose challenges for web designers who are accustomed to designing for left-to-right languages. Moreover, Arabic typography involves complex letterforms and ligatures that must be carefully considered to ensure readability and aesthetics.
