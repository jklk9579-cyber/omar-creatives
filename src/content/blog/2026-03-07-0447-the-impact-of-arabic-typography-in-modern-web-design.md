Arabic typography is a unique and complex script that requires special attention to detail. The Arabic language is written from right to left, which can pose challenges for web designers who are used to designing for left-to-right languages. However, with the right tools and expertise, Arabic typography can be a powerful tool for creating beautiful and engaging websites.
