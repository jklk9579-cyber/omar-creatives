When it comes to UX design, there are several factors to consider, including navigation, accessibility, and visual hierarchy. A clear and intuitive navigation system can help users find what they are looking for quickly and easily, while accessibility features such as font size and color contrast can ensure that your website or application is usable by everyone. Visual hierarchy, on the other hand, refers to the way in which visual elements are organized to guide the user's attention.
