## Top Web Design Trends in Egypt
Some of the most popular web design trends in Egypt include:
* **Minimalism**: Simple, clean, and intuitive designs that focus on the essential elements of a website.
* **Mobile-First Design**: With the majority of Egyptians accessing the internet through their mobile devices, mobile-first design has become a crucial aspect of web development.
* **Artificial Intelligence (AI) Integration**: AI-powered chatbots, personalized recommendations, and predictive analytics are becoming increasingly popular in Egyptian web design.
