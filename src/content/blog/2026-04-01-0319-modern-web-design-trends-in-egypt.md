## Key Trends Shaping the Egyptian Web Design Market
Some of the key trends shaping the Egyptian web design market include:
* **Mobile-first design**: With the majority of internet users in Egypt accessing the web through their mobile devices, mobile-first design has become a crucial aspect of web design in the country.
* **Artificial intelligence (AI) integration**: AI is being increasingly used in web design to enhance user experience, personalize content, and improve website functionality.
* **E-commerce solutions**: As online shopping continues to grow in popularity in Egypt, e-commerce solutions have become a key focus area for web designers and developers.
