Arabic typography is unique and requires special consideration when designing websites and digital platforms. The script is written from right to left, which can affect the overall layout and user experience. Moreover, the Arabic language has a distinct aesthetic, with intricate calligraphy and beautiful fonts that can enhance the visual appeal of a website.
