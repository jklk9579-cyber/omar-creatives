## Introduction
When it comes to building a startup, one of the most critical decisions you'll make is choosing the right tech stack. Your tech stack is the combination of programming languages, frameworks, libraries, and tools that you'll use to build and power your application. With so many options available, it can be overwhelming to decide which ones to use. In this post, we'll explore the key factors to consider when selecting a tech stack for your startup.

## Factors to Consider
There are several factors to consider when choosing a tech stack for your startup. These include:
* **Scalability**: Will your tech stack be able to handle increased traffic and usage as your startup grows?
* **Cost**: What are the costs associated with using each technology, including licensing fees, development costs, and maintenance costs?
* **Development Time**: How long will it take to develop your application using each technology?
* **Team Expertise**: What are the skills and expertise of your development team, and which technologies are they most familiar with?

## Popular Tech Stacks
Some popular tech stacks for startups include:
* **MEAN Stack**: MongoDB, Express.js, Angular.js, and Node.js
* **MERN Stack**: MongoDB, Express.js, React.js, and Node.js
* **LAMP Stack**: Linux, Apache, MySQL, and PHP
