## Key Trends in Modern Web Design
Several key trends are shaping the modern web design scene in Egypt:
- **Responsive Design**: With the majority of internet users accessing websites through mobile devices, responsive design has become a necessity. It ensures that websites are accessible and provide a seamless user experience across all devices.
- **Minimalism and Simple Navigation**: Minimalistic designs with intuitive navigation are gaining popularity. They improve user engagement by making it easier for visitors to find what they are looking for.
- **AI-Driven Personalization**: The use of AI to personalize user experiences is on the rise. AI algorithms can analyze user behavior and preferences, allowing for tailored content and recommendations.
- **Accessibility**: There is a growing emphasis on designing websites that are accessible to everyone, including those with disabilities. This involves incorporating features such as high contrast modes and screen reader compatibility.
