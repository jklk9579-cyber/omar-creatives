## Top Web Design Trends in Egypt
Some of the top web design trends in Egypt include:
* **Minimalism**: Simple, clean, and intuitive designs that focus on the user experience.
* **Mobile-First Design**: Designs that prioritize mobile devices to cater to the increasing number of mobile users in Egypt.
* **Interactive Elements**: Incorporating interactive elements such as animations, scrolling effects, and micro-interactions to enhance user engagement.
* **Accessibility**: Designing websites that are accessible to all users, regardless of their abilities or devices.
