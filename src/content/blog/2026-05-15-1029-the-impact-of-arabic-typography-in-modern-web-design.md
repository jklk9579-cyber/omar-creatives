## Challenges of Arabic Typography
Designing Arabic typography can be challenging, especially for non-Arabic speaking designers. Arabic script is written from right to left, which can be confusing for designers who are used to working with left-to-right scripts. Additionally, Arabic fonts can be complex, with intricate details and varying letterforms. However, with the right tools and expertise, these challenges can be overcome.
