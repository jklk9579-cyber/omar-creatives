Arabic typography is unique and requires special attention to detail. The Arabic script is written from right to left, and its letters are connected, making it a challenging task to design a website that is both aesthetically pleasing and functional. However, with the help of modern design tools and technologies, designers can now create stunning Arabic websites that meet the needs of their target audience.
